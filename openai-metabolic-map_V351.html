The Metabolic Map

This metabolic map shows the production chain that goes into frontier AI systems: minerals, energy, water, labour, and data circulating through planetary infrastructure. It is primarily based on evidence concerning OpenAI but is generalisable to comparable frontier AI companies.

Scaling laws are empirical relationships describing how AI model performance tends to improve as model size, training data, and compute increase. In practice, each new generation of frontier models has required substantially greater resources than the last with no clearly established ceiling yet in sight.

That dynamic produces three structural dispositions in the system:

  • Expansion — growth without sufficiency
  • Externalisation — costs distributed onto others
  • Opacification — information access fragmented

The map flows upstream → downstream across four phases: compute, data, model, deployment.
